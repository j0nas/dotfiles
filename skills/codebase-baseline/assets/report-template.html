Glossary

Intake queue
The project's name for the job table every new order passes through before a person sees it (jobs/intake/).
Autonomy level
How much agents may do without a person: 0 is none, 5 is unattended and self-healing.
Harness level
How well automated checks, review and rollback catch an agent's mistakes: 1 is rules in prose, 5 is sandboxed with auto-rollback.
